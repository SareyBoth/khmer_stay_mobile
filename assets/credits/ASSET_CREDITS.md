# KhmerStay asset credits

## Accommodation imagery

The four bundled accommodation scenes in `assets/images/` were generated specifically
for this fictional KhmerStay prototype with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on
2026-09-04. They do not depict real hotels, real payment requests, or affiliated brands.
The final generation prompt set is recorded in `IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.
