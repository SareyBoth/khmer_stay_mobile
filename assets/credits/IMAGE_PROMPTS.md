# KhmerStay image generation prompts

All four assets used the `photorealistic-natural` use case and were generated with
OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Each requested premium natural travel
photography, a landscape mobile-card crop, realistic materials, no people, no
logos, no text, no watermark, and no recognizable hotel brand.

## Siem Reap homestay

An original, locally owned Khmer raised wooden homestay in a tropical Siem Reap
garden at warm early morning, with a shaded veranda, rattan furniture, a lotus
pond, and restrained Angkor-region atmosphere. Warm ivory, deep temple brown,
muted sage, sandstone, and antique-gold light.

## Kampot riverside

An original boutique riverside guesthouse in Kampot, set among lush riverbank
greenery with misty Bokor foothills, a simple timber deck, pale sandstone, soft
linen, and a calm river in late-afternoon light.

## Kep coast

An original coastal homestay room in Kep opening to a terrace and quiet Cambodian
sea, with woven details, ivory linen, locally crafted furniture, palm shade, and
bright diffused morning light.

## Mondulkiri eco lodge

An original intimate timber eco-lodge cabin in misty Mondulkiri pine highlands,
with a deep porch, warm interior glow, native planting, and restrained
contemporary Khmer craft details at dawn.
